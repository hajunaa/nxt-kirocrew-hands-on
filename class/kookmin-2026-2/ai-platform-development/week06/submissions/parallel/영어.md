# Department AI Hands-on Meetup

## Summary

The department is hosting a hands-on meetup where students use AI together in practice. The session runs on October 16, 2026, from 18:30 to 20:00 (Asia/Seoul, KST) in the Meeting Room of the Student Union Building, with a capacity of 12 people and no participation fee. Students new to AI practice are welcome. English and Japanese guides are provided only so you can understand the event details; the guidance and the hands-on session on the day are conducted entirely in Korean. Please bring your own laptop and charger — Wi-Fi is available at the venue. To join, leave your name in the department group chat by 18:00 on October 14, 2026 (KST). All times are stated in Asia/Seoul (KST).
