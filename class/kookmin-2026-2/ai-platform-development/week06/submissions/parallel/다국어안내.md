## English

# Department AI Hands-On Meetup

## Summary

We are hosting a hands-on meetup where department friends can try using AI together. There is room for 12 people, and students who are new to AI practice are welcome to join. This English guide (along with the Japanese one) is provided only to help you understand the meeting details — the guidance and the hands-on practice on the day of the meetup are all conducted in Korean.

## Meeting Information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Schedule | October 16, 2026, 18:30–20:00 (Korea Time, Asia/Seoul) |
| Location | Student Union Hall meeting room |
| Capacity | 12 people |
| What to bring | Your personal laptop and charger |
| Wi-Fi | Available at the venue |
| Fee | None |
| Language of the meeting | Korean (both the announcement and the hands-on practice are conducted in Korean, regardless of this translation) |
| Who can join | Open to all, including students who are new to AI practice |

All times listed are in Korea Time. A Wi-Fi password or any separate connection method is not included in this guide.

## How to apply

If you would like to join, leave your name in the department group chat by 18:00 on October 14, 2026 (Korea Time). There is no separate application link — please apply according to the method described above after checking the date and deadline.

## 集まりの情報 / Meeting Information

| 項目 | 内容 |
| --- | --- |
| 日時 | 2026年10月16日 18:30～20:00（韓国時間 / Asia/Seoul） |
